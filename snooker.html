About Snooker

Build the highest break you can. Red, then a colour, then red again — colours are re-spotted while reds remain, and when the table clears it is the colours in order: yellow to black.

🎯 How to Play

Aim with the mouse, drag back for power, release to shoot. Reds score 1 and colours 2 to 7; hitting or potting the wrong ball is a foul that ends your break and costs at least 4 points.

⭐ Features

  • Authentic red–colour sequence and final-colour clearance
  • Break and total scoring with a saved best break
