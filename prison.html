About Prison Escape Run

Night falls on the prison yard and you have one plan: three tools hidden around the grounds — a key, wire cutters and a rope — and a gate at the north fence. Four guards sweep the dark with torchlight cones. Grab the gear, mind the light, and vanish.

🎯 How to Play

Move with WASD, the arrow keys or the pad. Walk over an item to pocket it. If a guard's torch cone catches you, that's a strike and you're marched back to your cell (items stay with you). Three strikes end the run. With all three items, reach the glowing gate at the top to escape — fastest escape time is saved.

⭐ Features

  • Four patrolling guards with sweeping torchlight vision cones
  • Strike system with cell restarts — items are never lost
