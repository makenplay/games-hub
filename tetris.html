About Block Puzzle (Tetris)

The greatest puzzle game of all time. Stack the falling tetrominoes, complete full lines to clear them, and survive as the pieces fall faster and faster.

🎯 How to Play

Move with the arrow keys (or on-screen buttons), UP to rotate, DOWN for a soft drop and SPACE for a hard drop. Clear up to four lines at once for a TETRIS bonus.

⭐ Features

  • All seven classic tetrominoes with line-clear scoring
  • Level-ups raise the speed — how long can you last?
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
