About Paper Territory

The land-grab sensation. You own a small patch of the map; venture out and you leave a fragile trail behind you. Complete a loop back into your own land and everything inside it becomes yours — but while you're out, one touch on your trail from anyone ends you.

🎯 How to Play

Steer with the arrow keys, WASD or the pad. Inside your own color you are safe; outside it you draw a trail. Return home to claim the enclosed area — including any rival land inside your loop. Slice a rival's exposed trail to eliminate them and take the lead. First to 30% of the map wins.

  • Trail-cutting kills and steal-their-land loops
