About Water Sort Puzzle

The satisfying colour-sorting puzzle. Each tube holds four layers of coloured water, shuffled between tubes. Pour the top layer of one tube into another — but only onto the same colour, and only when there's room. Sort every colour into its own tube to clear the level.

🎯 How to Play

Tap a tube to lift it, then tap the tube you want to pour into. The whole matching top layer pours at once, up to the space available. The two empty tubes are your workspace — plan around them. Really stuck? HINT runs the built-in solver and flashes a pour that lies on a true solution path. Every level here is solver-verified before it ships, so a solution always exists — and PAR shows the shortest one we found.

⭐ Features
