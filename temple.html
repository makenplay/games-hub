About Temple Dash

A pseudo-3D endless runner through ancient ruins. Sprint down the temple path, leap the barriers, slide under the traps and dodge the blocks while grabbing every coin.

🎯 How to Play

Use the arrow keys or swipe to change lanes, jump and slide. The run speeds up the longer you survive — one mistake ends it, so keep your eyes on the next obstacle.

⭐ Features

  • Fast lane-based 3D-style running action
  • Coins, distance score and rising speed
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
