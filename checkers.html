About Checkers

Classic draughts against the computer, with the real rules: captures are forced, multi-jumps chain, and a piece that reaches the far row is crowned king and can move backwards. You play red, moving up the board; take all twelve black pieces — or block them so they can't move — to win.

🎯 How to Play

Tap one of your red pieces: its legal squares glow (when a capture exists, only capturing pieces can move — that's the rule). Tap a glowing square to move. After a jump, if the same piece can jump again it stays selected — keep going. The CPU thinks two moves deep and never misses a forced capture, so guard your pieces and set traps.
