About Word Search

The classic pencil-and-paper hunt, against the clock. Eight themed words hide in every 10×10 grid — across, down and diagonal, forwards and backwards. Drag across a word's letters to claim it; the list above the grid ticks each one off. Finish the round and a fresh theme deals in.

🎯 How to Play

Press START ROUND, read the word list, then press and drag from a word's first letter to its last (mouse or touch). A correct drag locks the word in green and scores its length × 10 plus whatever is left of your speed bonus. Stuck? HINT flashes a word's first letter for 40 points. Themes rotate: animals, space, food, sports, the sea and more.

⭐ Features

  • Six rotating word themes with a fresh generated grid every round
  • Words hide in 4 directions, forwards and backwards
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
