About Battle Arena Royale

Twenty-four fighters, one island, one winner. Drop in with your fists, scavenge pistols, SMGs, rifles and shotguns from the field, grab medkits, and keep inside the shrinking safe zone while 23 AI rivals loot and fight each other down to the last duel.

🎯 How to Play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys; aim with the mouse and click or hold to fire (on touch, FIRE aims at the nearest enemy). Walk over loot to pick it up — better guns replace weaker ones automatically. Press H or HEAL to use a medkit. The zone shrinks in phases; outside it you bleed health fast. Be the last fighter standing.

  • Five weapon tiers, medkits and a phased shrinking zone
