About Monster Truck

A stadium freestyle show in a giant-wheeled truck. Twelve sacrificial cars are lined up in rows and stacks around two ramps — crush every one you can, catch the biggest air you dare, and keep the crowd's score climbing for two full minutes.

🎯 How to Play

Drive with GAS (→) and REV (←), tilting with the pedals in the air. Roll over a car to crush it flat for 50 points — crushed cars stay crushed. Hit a ramp fast and your air time scores up to 150 points; land on your wheels to keep it. The show ends at 0:00.

⭐ Features

  • Big-wheel physics with crushable cars that squash flat
  • Two ramps, twelve cars and an air-time scoring system
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
