About Bubble Pop Mania

A level-based bubble shooter with a shot budget. Each level hangs a fresh pattern of bubbles from the ceiling and gives you a limited number of shots — clear every last bubble to advance, and the more shots you save, the more stars you earn. Bank shots off the walls, aim for the clusters that hold everything else up, and watch whole branches rain down.

🎯 How to Play

Move the mouse or drag on the board to aim the dotted guide line, then click or release to fire. Your bubble sticks where it lands; if it touches 2 or more bubbles of the same colour, the whole group pops. Any bubbles left hanging with no connection to the ceiling drop for double points. Run out of shots with bubbles remaining and the level is lost. SWAP trades your loaded bubble for the next one.

⭐ Features

  • Endless levels with growing patterns, more colours and tighter shot budgets
  • Drop bonuses, wall bounces and a 3-star rating on every level
