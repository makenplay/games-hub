About Flappy Wings

The one-tap arcade challenge everyone loves to hate. Pilot your little bird through endless rows of pipes — one tap to flap, perfect timing to survive.

🎯 How to Play

Click, tap or press SPACE to flap upward; gravity pulls you down the rest of the time. Pass through the gap in each pipe pair to score, and thread the needle as the speed builds.
