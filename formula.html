About Formula Racing

A full grand prix circuit in miniature: one long loop with sweepers and a hairpin, three laps, and three rival cars that race the same racing line. Positions are live, lap times are kept, and the grass is exactly as slow as it should be.

🎯 How to Play

Accelerate with W/↑ or GAS, brake with S/↓, steer with A/D. Pass each gold checkpoint in order (the arrow points to the next one) to advance your lap. The race ends after 3 laps — finish in the top 3 for a podium, win it for the trophy count.

⭐ Features

  • A real closed circuit with checkpoint-verified laps — no cutting corners
  • Three AI rivals with individual pace, live positions and lap timing
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
