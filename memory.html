About Memory Cards

Test your brain with the classic card-matching game. Flip two cards at a time, remember where every picture hides, and clear the whole board in as few moves as you can.

🎯 How to Play

Click or tap a card to flip it. Match all the pairs to win — your moves and time are counted, so a sharp memory means a better score.

⭐ Features

  • Clean flip animations and move/time tracking
  • A real workout for focus and short-term memory
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
