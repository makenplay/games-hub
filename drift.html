About Drift Racing

Ninety seconds in a walled drift arena. Points come from one thing only: sliding. The bigger your drift angle and the faster you're moving, the faster the counter climbs — and drifts chained within 2.5 seconds multiply everything you bank.

🎯 How to Play

Accelerate with W/↑ or GAS, steer with A/D, brake with S/↓. Enter a corner fast, steer hard, and the rear of the car steps out — keep the slide alive with throttle and opposite steering. Yellow zones double drift points. Banking a drift adds its score × your chain multiplier; spinning out or smacking a cone resets the chain.

⭐ Features

  • True drift physics — velocity and heading tracked separately with a grip model
  • Chain multiplier system with double-score drift zones
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
