About Banana Rush

A giggling yellow dash through the lab district: three lanes of barriers, beams and runaway carts, with bananas scattered everywhere. What makes this runner different is the mission board — run the distance, hoard the bananas, shave past obstacles — because every finished mission permanently raises your score multiplier.

🎯 How to Play

Switch lanes with ◀ ▶, jump with ⬆, slide with ⬇ (arrows, WASD or the pad). Bananas score points times your multiplier. Watch the mission line above the road: complete all three missions to level up your multiplier (up to ×5), then a harder set appears. Grab the magnet to vacuum up bananas automatically.

⭐ Features

  • Mission system with a permanent score multiplier up to ×5
  • Banana magnet power-up and near-miss dodge bonuses
