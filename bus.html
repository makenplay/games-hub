About Bus Simulator

A driving game with a job to do. Drive your bus route, pull into each stop at the right speed and in the right lane, and pick up every passenger waiting.

🎯 How to Play

Accelerate and brake with the arrow keys and steer between lanes. To serve a stop, be in the right lane at 30 km/h or slower as you pass it — too fast and your passengers stay behind.

⭐ Features

  • A real objective: serve every stop on the route
  • Speed-and-lane skill driving, not just racing
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
