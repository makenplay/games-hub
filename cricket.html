About Cricket Batting

Ten balls, three wickets, one target to chase. The bowler mixes express pace, late swing and turning spin — your only job is to watch the ball onto the bat and swing at exactly the right moment.

🎯 How to Play

Click, tap, or press SPACE to swing as the ball reaches the crease. Dead-centre timing sends it for six; slightly early or late gives fours, twos or singles — and a wild miss means the stumps are shattered and a wicket falls. Beat the target before the balls or wickets run out.

⭐ Features

  • Three ball types — pace, late swing and spin — each with its own timing
  • A real chase: target, wickets and balls all counting down together
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
