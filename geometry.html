About Geometry Jump

The one-button rhythm platformer that defined a genre. Your cube runs on its own — you just jump. Spikes, stacked blocks, double spikes and bounce pads are laid out to the beat; every crash restarts the level and teaches you one more pattern.

🎯 How to Play

Press SPACE, click, tap the screen or hit JUMP to leap. Hold nothing, steer nothing — timing is everything. Land on top of blocks to cross gaps, bounce on yellow pads automatically, and never touch a spike. Your best percentage is saved; reaching 100% completes the level.

⭐ Features

  • A hand-laid obstacle course with singles, doubles and stair stacks
  • Instant restarts with attempt counting and best-% tracking
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
