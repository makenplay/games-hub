About Cricket Bowling

The other half of cricket — and the thinking half. You have a total to defend in 10 balls. Pick where each delivery pitches, choose from yorker, good length, bouncer or slower ball, and outsmart a batsman who learns your habits.

🎯 How to Play

Click on the pitch to place your target marker (line and length), pick a delivery type, then press BOWL. Tight lines at the stumps are hard to score off; stray wide or full and you will be punished. Bowl the same ball too often and the batsman reads it — mix it up to take all 3 wickets or defend your total.

  • Four real delivery types with genuine risk and reward
  • A batsman AI that adapts to repeated deliveries
