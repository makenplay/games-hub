About Sudoku

The world's favourite number puzzle, generated fresh every game — and every puzzle is verified to have exactly one solution, so logic alone always wins. Fill the 9×9 grid so that every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

🎯 How to Play

Choose Easy, Medium or Hard and press NEW GAME. Tap any empty cell, then tap a number on the pad (or type 1–9). A correct number locks in; a wrong one flashes red and counts as a mistake — three mistakes end the game. HINT fills one correct cell when you're stuck. Beat your best time on each difficulty.

⭐ Features

  • Fresh unique-solution puzzles on three difficulties, generated on the spot
  • Row, column and box highlighting plus same-number tracking
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
