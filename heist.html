About Heist Run

One robber, one bank, 150 seconds. Eight money bags sit in the vault rooms while three guards pace their patrols, sweeping yellow vision cones across the floor. Thread the gaps, fill your bags — every bag makes you richer and slower — then leg it to the getaway van.

🎯 How to Play

Move with WASD, the arrow keys or the pad. Walk over a money bag to grab it. If a guard's vision cone touches you he gives chase; if he catches you, you're dumped back at the entrance and the alarm level rises (guards patrol faster). Grab all 8 bags and stand at the van before the timer ends to complete the heist.

⭐ Features

  • Real stealth vision cones with patrol, chase and search behaviour
  • Risk-reward carrying: more loot, slower legs, bigger score
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
