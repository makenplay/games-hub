About Ludo

The family board classic, in full. Race all four of your tokens around the board against three computer players — roll a six to leave base, capture rivals to send them home, and use the safe stars wisely.

🎯 How to Play

Tap ROLL DICE, then tap one of your glowing tokens to move it. Captures, sixes and bringing a token home all earn an extra roll. First to bring all four tokens home wins the game.

⭐ Features

  • The complete 52-cell board with safe stars and home columns
  • Three computer opponents with real capture-and-escape strategy
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
