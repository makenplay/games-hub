About Crossy Road

Why did the chicken cross the road? To beat your high score. Hop across endless lanes of traffic, railway-fast cars and log-filled rivers in this voxel-style classic, rebuilt for the browser. One mistimed hop ends the run.

🎯 How to Play

Hop with the arrow keys, WASD or the on-screen pad: forward scores a point, sideways dodges, backward retreats (careful — the camera keeps creeping up). On rivers, land on logs and ride them, but don't drift off the edge or fall in the water. Grab coins for +5.

⭐ Features

  • Endless generated lanes — roads, rivers and rest grass
  • Log-riding physics and a camera that punishes dawdling
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
