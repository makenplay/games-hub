About Asteroids

The neon vector classic. You are one small ship with real inertia in an endless rock field: every boulder you blast splits into faster, smaller fragments until the tiniest ones — worth the most points — pop out of existence.

🎯 How to Play

Rotate with A/D or the arrow keys, thrust with W/↑ (your ship keeps drifting — tap thrust, don't hold it), and fire with SPACE. Clear every rock to advance a wave; each wave adds another large asteroid. Three ships; collisions with any rock cost one.

⭐ Features

  • True momentum physics with wrap-around space
  • Splitting rocks with escalating waves
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
