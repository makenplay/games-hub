About Helicopter Shooting

A side-scrolling combat flight through a hostile valley. Your chopper sinks when you let go and climbs when you hold — thread it between the rock ceiling and the ground while enemy turrets and helicopters try to bring you down.

🎯 How to Play

Hold the mouse button, your finger, or W/↑ to climb; release to descend. Fire with F, SPACE, or the FIRE button. Ground turrets take one missile, enemy choppers two. Distance and kills both score — survive as far as you can with 3 lives.

⭐ Features

  • One-touch lift physics that are easy to learn and hard to master
  • Ground turrets and enemy helicopters with return fire
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
