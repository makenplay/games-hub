About Crossword Mini

All the pleasure of a crossword, none of the Sunday-paper marathon. Three hand-made 6×6 mini puzzles — the sky, the sea and the kitchen — each with interlocking across and down clues. Fill the grid, hit CHECK to lock in your correct letters, and clear all three for the full score.

🎯 How to Play

Tap a clue in the list (or any white square) to select that word — it glows gold. Type with the on-screen keyboard or your real one; the cursor walks along the word as you type, and tapping a crossing square again switches direction. CHECK scores every correct letter +10 and locks it green; wrong letters flash red and clear. HINT reveals one letter for 15 points.

⭐ Features

  • Three hand-crafted interlocking puzzles with fair, friendly clues
  • Tap-to-switch direction at crossings, just like a real crossword
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
