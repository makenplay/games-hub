About Diep Tanks

The geometric tank arena. Roam a grid world blasting floating shapes for XP; every level grants an upgrade point to spend on damage, fire rate, speed or health. Rival AI tanks roam the same fields — outgrow them before they outgrow you.

🎯 How to Play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys; your cannon aims at the mouse (on touch, it aims where you tap and auto-fires). Destroy squares, triangles and pentagons for XP and score. On each level-up, pick one upgrade from the green buttons. Your health regenerates slowly — ram damage is real, so keep your distance.

⭐ Features

  • Full XP/level system with 4 spendable upgrade tracks
  • 3 rival tanks that farm, fight and level alongside you
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
