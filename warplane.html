About War Plane Shooting

A classic vertical sky-war shooter. Your lone fighter climbs into enemy airspace where dive-bombers, weaving fighters and heavy bombers keep coming in thicker waves — shoot them down, collect the stars they drop, and keep upgrading your guns.

🎯 How to Play

Steer with the mouse, your finger, or the arrow keys/WASD — the cannons fire automatically. Fighters take one hit, weavers two, bombers four. Downed bombers drop ⭐ power-ups that raise your gun level (up to twin triple-shot); getting hit costs a gun level, and a collision costs a life.

⭐ Features

  • Three enemy types with different flight patterns and toughness
  • A 3-level weapon upgrade system earned in the air
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
