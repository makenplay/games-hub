About Sliding Puzzle

The timeless sliding puzzle — the famous 15-puzzle and its brothers. Numbered tiles are shuffled on the board with one empty space; slide tiles into the gap, one at a time, until they read 1 to 15 in order with the blank in the corner. Unlike the cheap versions, every shuffle here is made by sliding real moves from the solved board, so a solution always exists.

🎯 How to Play

Tap any tile beside the empty space and it slides in. Arrow keys work too. Plan ahead: finish the top row first, then the left column, and shrink the puzzle down to a 2×2 corner. Tiles already in their home square wear a white frame. Race the clock on 3×3, take the classic 4×4 challenge, or go big on 5×5 — your fewest-moves best for each size is saved.
