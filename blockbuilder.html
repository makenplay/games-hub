About Block Builder

A pocket blocky sandbox in the browser: rolling hills, oak trees and stone under your feet. Mine any block into your pocket, then place blocks back to build towers, bridges, houses — anything. Your world saves automatically in this browser, so your castle is still here tomorrow.

🎯 How to Play

Walk with A/D or the arrows and jump with W or SPACE. Click or tap any block within reach to mine it. Choose a block in the hotbar (or keys 1–7) and click an empty cell next to solid ground to place it. Placing spends blocks from your pocket, so mine before you build. NEW WORLD generates a fresh landscape.

⭐ Features

  • Procedural hills-and-trees world, 90 blocks wide
  • 7 block types with a real mine → carry → build inventory loop
  • Your world auto-saves — free to play, no download, no sign-up
