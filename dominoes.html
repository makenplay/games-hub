About Dominoes

The classic double-6 draw game against the computer. Seven tiles each, a growing chain on the table, and one simple rule: match an open end. Empty your hand to score everything left in the CPU's hand — or, if the game blocks, the lower pip count wins the difference. First to 100 takes the match.

🎯 How to Play

Your playable tiles glow gold. Click one to play it — if it fits both ends you'll be asked which side; if it fits only one, it goes there automatically. No matching tile? DRAW from the boneyard until you find one (drawing ends your turn once you play). The CPU plays by the same rules. Watch the open ends and hold onto your doubles — they're the hardest tiles to shed.
