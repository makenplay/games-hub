About Maze Runner Puzzle

Every level is a brand-new maze, generated the moment you start — no two runs are ever the same. You are the glowing runner in the top-left corner; the chequered flag waits in the bottom-right. Three golden stars hide along the way, and the maze grows bigger and twistier with every level you clear.

🎯 How to Play

Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad. The clock starts on your first step. The BEST PATH counter shows the shortest possible route in steps — finish close to it for a perfect run. Clearing a level unlocks the next, larger maze; your best total time for each level size is saved. Dead end? Backtrack and try the other fork — a perfect maze always has exactly one route between any two points, so every wrong turn teaches you the map.
