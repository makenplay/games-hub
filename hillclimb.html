About Hill Climb

The endless hill-driving classic. One jeep, infinite countryside, and a fuel gauge that never stops falling. Feather the pedals over the crests, catch air without flipping, and chase the next fuel can before the engine coughs out.

🎯 How to Play

Hold GAS (D / → or the green button) to drive and BRAKE (A / ←) to slow down or reverse. In the air, GAS tilts the jeep backward and BRAKE tilts it forward — use that to land safely and complete flips for +100. Collect coins (+10) and fuel cans to keep going. The run ends when you flip onto the roof or run dry.

⭐ Features

  • Endless procedurally-rolling hills that get steeper the further you go
  • Fuel management, coin pickups and airborne flip bonuses
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
