About 8 Ball Pool

Proper 8-ball against the computer. The first ball potted decides solids and stripes, fouls give ball-in-hand, and the black only counts once your group is cleared.

🎯 How to Play

Aim with the mouse, drag back for power and release to shoot. Pot all of your group, then sink the 8 to win — sink it early and the game is lost on the spot.
