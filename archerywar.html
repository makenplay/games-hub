About Stickman Archery War

Two stickman archers, two cliffs, one windy canyon. Take turns loosing arrows across the gap — drag back to set your angle and power, read the dotted preview, and correct for the wind that pushes every arrow sideways. Headshots deal 50, body shots 30, leg shots 20. Drop the enemy's health to zero before he drops yours.

🎯 How to Play

Press and drag anywhere: an aiming line stretches from your bow, and the dotted curve previews the arrow's flight. Release to loose. The wind value changes every turn — an arrow drifting right needs aim to the left. The enemy is a real ballistic shot too: he solves the same physics you do, and his aim sharpens every round he survives, so finish duels quickly. Win and the next round starts on a fresh canyon with a healthier, sharper opponent.

⭐ Features

  • Zone damage — head 50, body 30, legs 20
  • An AI duelist whose aim improves round by round
