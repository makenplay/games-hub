About Soccer 3v3

Two minutes of frantic top-down football. Three-a-side on a tight pitch means the ball is never far from a goal — dribble past the red shirts, shoot early, and track back fast when the CPU breaks away.

🎯 How to Play

Move with the arrow keys, WASD or the on-screen pad — you always control the blue player with the ring. Run into the ball to dribble it; press SPACE (or SHOOT) to fire at goal. Your teammates and keeper position themselves automatically. Most goals when the 2-minute clock ends wins the trophy.

  • A CPU team that dribbles, shoots and defends its box
