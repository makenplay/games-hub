About Agar Cells

The eat-or-be-eaten cell arena. You are one hungry cell in a petri dish of pellets and rival cells — some tiny snacks, some roaming giants. Graze to grow, hunt anything smaller, and never let a bigger cell catch you.

🎯 How to Play

Steer with the mouse or touch — your cell drifts toward your pointer. Eat colored pellets to gain mass, and swallow rival cells once you are clearly bigger than them (about 12% more radius and good overlap). Your rank updates live; finish a run as #1 to rule the dish.

⭐ Features

  • 9 AI cells that graze, hunt and flee by size
  • Real agar rules — size-gated swallowing, size-scaled speed
