About Piano Tiles

The finger-tapping phenomenon: an endless river of black tiles flows down four lanes and your only job is to tap every single one — and nothing else. Each correct tap plays the next note of the melody; one mistake and the music stops.

🎯 How to Play

Tap or click the black tiles before they slide off the bottom, or use the D F J K keys for the four lanes. Never tap a white space, and never let a black tile pass. The scroll speed rises steadily with your score — stay calm, find the rhythm.

⭐ Features

  • A real melody note-by-note — Für Elise — played by your taps
  • Endless mode with a steadily rising speed curve
