About Drag Racing

Pure reaction and timing. A Christmas-tree start light, five gears, and a rival in the next lane who does not make many mistakes. Win the launch, hit every shift in the green, and the quarter mile is yours.

🎯 How to Play

Press STAGE to light the tree: three ambers, then green. Hold GAS only after green — launching early is a red-light foul and the race is over. Keep GAS held and press SHIFT (Space or the button) each time the RPM needle reaches the green zone; perfect shifts keep acceleration strong, late shifts hit the limiter and bog you down.
