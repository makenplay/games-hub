About Sniper Shooting

Ninety seconds, six windows, one rifle. Enemy targets pop up across the building opposite and duck away in a heartbeat — your job is to spot them through the scope, settle your breathing and make every shot count.

🎯 How to Play

Move the mouse (or drag on mobile) to aim the scope — the lens magnifies whatever it covers. Click or tap to fire: headshots score 150, body hits 100. Your crosshair sways while you move; hold it still for a moment and the sway fades for a clean shot. Six rounds per magazine — press R or the RELOAD button when empty.

  • Headshot bonuses and accuracy pressure against the clock
