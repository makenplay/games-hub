About Pac Maze

The arcade legend in maze form. Gobble every pellet in the maze while four ghosts hunt you down — grab a power pellet and turn the tables on them.

🎯 How to Play

Steer with the arrow keys, WASD or swipe. Clear all pellets to finish the maze; power pellets make ghosts edible for a few seconds, and fruit bonuses appear near the ghost house.

⭐ Features

  • Four ghosts with classic hunting behaviour
  • Power pellets, fruit bonuses and level progression
