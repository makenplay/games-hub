About Parking Master

Six parking lots, one marked bay, and rows of cones and parked cars that punish every careless swing. This is precision driving at walking pace — plan your arc, use reverse properly, and stop dead-centre and straight.

🎯 How to Play

Accelerate with W/↑, reverse with S/↓, and steer with A/D (the car turns only while moving, and steering reverses when backing up — like a real car). Roll fully into the green bay, straighten up, and stop. Parking quality earns 1–3 stars: fast and bump-free is worth the most.

⭐ Features

  • Six lots from an easy straight-in bay to a tight parallel park
  • True Ackermann-style steering feel with cones and parked cars as obstacles
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
