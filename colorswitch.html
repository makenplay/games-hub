About Color Switch

The hypnotic color-matching climber. Your ball bounces upward through endless spinning wheels, and it may only touch the arc segment painted its own color. Every wheel hides a rainbow star that repaints the ball — so the color you need is always changing.

🎯 How to Play

Tap, click or press SPACE to bounce the ball up; gravity pulls it back down. Time each rise so the ball crosses the wheel's ring exactly where the matching color passes. Touch a wrong color and the run ends. Score one point per wheel cleared.

  • Endless wheels that spin faster the higher you climb
  • Color-changer stars that keep every run unpredictable
