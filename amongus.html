About Crewmate Tasks

The spaceship chore-run, distilled: you are the lone crewmate on duty and five stations need fixing — wiring, card swipe, asteroid watch, fuel and a medbay scan. An impostor is on the way; finish the green task bar before the countdown dies and the ship is saved.

🎯 How to Play

Walk with the arrow keys, WASD or the pad. Stand at a glowing station and press E or USE to open its mini-task: each one is different — sequence memory, timing, rapid tapping, target clicking or pure patience. Complete all five before the 150-second impostor timer runs out.
