About Zombie Shooting

The dead are coming in waves and your barricade is all that stands between them and you. Hold the line with careful aim: pick off the runners first, drop the brutes with headshots, and never let the horde reach the fence.

🎯 How to Play

Click or tap a zombie to shoot it. Body shots take two hits (five for the big brutes), a headshot kills instantly and scores double. You carry 12 rounds — press R or RELOAD before you run dry at the worst moment. Every wave is faster and thicker than the last; if the barricade health hits zero, the run is over.

⭐ Features

  • Three zombie types — shamblers, runners and armoured brutes
  • Endless escalating waves with a barricade to defend
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
