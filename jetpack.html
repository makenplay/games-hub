About Jetpack Flight

Strap on the lab's experimental jetpack and blast down an endless corridor of electric zappers, homing missiles and coin trails. One button is all you get — hold to climb, release to dive — and one mistake ends the run.

🎯 How to Play

Press and hold the mouse, touch or SPACE to fire the jetpack; release to fall. Thread between the sparking zappers, watch for the missile warning sign and move out of its line, and sweep through coin arcs for bonus points. Your score is distance plus coins — how far can you fly?

⭐ Features

  • Three hazards: static zappers, angled zappers and warned missiles
  • Speed ramps up the farther you fly — pure one-button skill
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
