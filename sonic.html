About Fast Runner

A momentum runner in the classic hedgehog style: rolling green hills, golden rings, spring pads — and a full loop-de-loop that only works if you hit it at serious speed. Two zones, one goal post, zero brakes.

🎯 How to Play

Hold ▶ (D) to accelerate — speed builds gradually and hills feed it. Jump with SPACE/W. Collect rings: they are your shield, and taking a hit scatters them instead of ending the run; a hit with zero rings ends it. Springs launch you to high routes full of rings. Reach the goal post to clear the zone.

⭐ Features

  • Momentum physics — downhill speed, uphill drag, airborne control
  • A working loop-de-loop with a minimum entry speed
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
