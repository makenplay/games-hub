About Gold Run Chase

The robber just emptied the vault and he's sprinting through downtown with your gold. Chase him across three lanes of traffic — leap the barriers, slide under the signs, dodge the cars — scooping up the gold bars he drops. Fill the catch meter and he's yours… then his faster brother takes off.

🎯 How to Play

Switch lanes with ◀ ▶, jump with ⬆ and slide with ⬇ (arrow keys, WASD or the pad). Gold bars add score and fill the red catch meter; distance fills it slowly too. When the meter fills you catch the robber for a +500 bonus and the chase restarts faster. One crash ends the run.

⭐ Features

  • A chase, not just a run — catch robbers for escalating bonuses
  • City traffic: cars, barriers and overhead signs in 3 lanes
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
