About Bowling

A full ten frames of proper ten-pin bowling with real scoring — strikes, spares, and a tenth frame that can make or break the game. Find the pocket, manage your hook, and chase the perfect 300.

🎯 How to Play

Set your stance with Position, point the ball with Aim, then choose Power and Hook (the sideways curve as the ball travels). Press BOWL and watch it run. Knock the head pin dead-centre with full power and you risk a split; slide the ball into the pocket beside it and strikes follow.
