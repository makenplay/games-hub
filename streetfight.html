About Street Fighting

The classic side-scrolling beat-'em-up. Your neighbourhood has a thug problem on three streets — walk them, fight through punks, bikers and bruisers, grab the burgers they drop, and finish the boss who runs the whole crew.

🎯 How to Play

Move in all four directions (WASD or the buttons) across the street's depth, punch with J and kick with K. Enemies approach from both sides — face them to hit them. Unanswered hits build a combo multiplier for bonus score; taking a hit resets it. Clear every thug on a street, then walk off the right edge to advance.

  • Three streets, three enemy types and a heavyweight boss finale
  • Combo multiplier scoring and health pickups dropped by fallen thugs
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
