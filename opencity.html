About Open City Driving

A pocket-sized open world: a full city grid of avenues and blocks that is yours to cruise. Checkpoint missions pay cash with time bonuses, loose cash bags litter the streets — and every cone you flatten or pedestrian you scare brings the police closer.

🎯 How to Play

Drive with WASD or the arrow keys. Follow the gold beacon to each mission checkpoint before its timer ends; faster arrivals pay more. Wanted stars rise with chaos and bring chasing police cars — if a cop stays glued to your bumper the bust meter fills and you lose your cash. Break line of sight and lay low to cool the heat.

⭐ Features

  • A 2400-unit open city with solid buildings and lit streets
  • Endless checkpoint missions, cash pickups and a wanted system
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
