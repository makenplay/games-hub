About Ball Sort Puzzle

The ball-sorting favourite: glossy coloured balls shuffled between glass tubes, four to a tube. Pour the top balls from one tube into another — only onto the same colour, and only when there's room — until every tube holds balls of a single colour.

🎯 How to Play

Tap a tube to lift it, then tap where the balls should go. All the matching top balls pour together, up to the space in the destination tube. The two empty tubes are your manoeuvring room. Made a mess? UNDO rolls back your last pour — no penalty, use it as often as you like and learn the line. Every level is solver-verified, and PAR shows the shortest solution we found.

⭐ Features

  • Three solver-verified levels — 5, 7 and 9 colours
  • Unlimited undo, move counter, timer and saved best scores
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
