About Slingshot Birds

Pull back, aim… and let fly. Launch birds from the slingshot to smash the block towers and pop every pig hiding inside — three levels of increasing chaos.

🎯 How to Play

Drag the bird back on the slingshot and release to launch; the dotted line shows your arc. Wood breaks easily, stone is tough — drop blocks onto pigs to squash them for bonus falls.
