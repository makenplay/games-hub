About Tank Battle

The timeless arena classic. You are the lone yellow tank defending the eagle at the bottom of the field while enemy armour pours in from the top — blast through brick walls to make your own routes, and never let them reach the base.

🎯 How to Play

Drive with the arrow keys or WASD (or the on-screen pad on mobile) and fire with SPACE or the FIRE button. Each stage sends a set number of enemy tanks; destroy them all to advance. Enemy fire and contact cost you a life — and if a shell hits the eagle, the game ends instantly, so guard it with your hull.

⭐ Features

  • Destructible brick terrain and indestructible steel, just like the classic
  • Escalating stages with faster, tougher enemy tanks
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
