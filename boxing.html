About Boxing

Three rounds against Bruno, a bruiser who watches your guard. Work the jab to score, save the cross for when he is open, and get your block up when his shoulder dips — because his counter-hook is coming.

🎯 How to Play

Jab with J or the JAB button (fast, light), throw the Cross with K (slow, heavy), and hold BLOCK with L/S or the block button to cut incoming damage. Punches cost stamina; exhausted punches do almost nothing, so let the bar recover. Knock Bruno out, or outscore him on health when the final bell rings.

⭐ Features

  • A thinking opponent who telegraphs, blocks and counter-punches
  • Stamina management across 3 full rounds, KO or points decision
