About Carrom Pool

The beloved South-Asian board game. Flick your striker to pocket the coins into the corner pockets — and remember the queen: she is worth 50, but only if you cover her with your very next coin.

🎯 How to Play

Slide the striker along the bottom line, then pull back and release to shoot. White coins score 10, black 20; pocketing the striker is a foul that costs 25 and returns an uncovered queen to the centre.
