About Draw Bridge

Your buddy needs to roll home, but the road is broken. You get one pot of ink and one stroke: draw a bridge from cliff to cliff, press ROLL, and watch physics decide whether your engineering holds. Too steep and he stalls; too saggy and he dips into the water; just right and he cruises to the flag.

🎯 How to Play

Press and drag on the canvas to draw one continuous bridge — start it on the left cliff and end it on the right cliff so both ends are anchored. The INK meter shows how much line you have left. Press ROLL and the buddy sets off: he keeps his speed on the flat, climbs gentle slopes, and rolls back down anything too steep. Four crossings, each wider and trickier than the last — one has a rock island in the middle, one makes you land on a lower ledge. CLEAR INK wipes your stroke and refunds the pot.

⭐ Features

  • Real draw-your-own physics: your exact line becomes the road
  • Four handcrafted crossings with an ink budget per level
