About Doodle Jump

The endless vertical climber that launched a thousand imitators. Your doodler bounces on its own — you just steer. Green platforms are safe, blue ones slide side to side, brown ones snap under your feet, and springs catapult you skyward.

🎯 How to Play

Steer with A/D, the arrow keys or the on-screen buttons. Landing on a platform bounces you automatically. The screen wraps — exit left, enter right. The camera only moves up, so every missed platform can end the run. Score grows with every metre climbed.

⭐ Features

  • Three platform types plus spring boosts
  • Endless, ever-faster climbing with a saved best score
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
