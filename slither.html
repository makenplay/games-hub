About Slither Worm

The arena worm phenomenon. You start tiny in a dark field of glowing food, sharing the space with rival worms. Every orb makes you longer; every rival who crashes into your body bursts into a feast of orbs. Grow to rule the leaderboard — but one touch of your head on any body, including your own, ends it.

🎯 How to Play

Steer with the mouse, touch, or arrow keys. Eat the glowing orbs to grow. Hold BOOST (or the ⚡ button) for a burst of speed that slowly burns your length — perfect for cutting across a rival's path so they crash into you. Avoid every body and the red arena edge.
