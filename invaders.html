About Space Invaders

The arcade legend, faithfully rebuilt: five rows of invaders march side to side, dropping a step closer with every pass — and the fewer remain, the faster they move. Four green shields stand between you and their bombs, but every shot chews them away.

🎯 How to Play

Slide your cannon with A/D or the arrow buttons and fire with SPACE (one shot at a time, like the original). Clear the whole formation to advance a wave; each new wave starts one row lower. If an invader lands, or your three cannons fall, the game ends.

⭐ Features

  • Authentic marching formation that speeds up as it thins
  • Destructible shields and a bonus UFO flyby
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
