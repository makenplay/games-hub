About Jewel Quest

A match-3 with stakes: every level gives you 25 moves to hit a rising score target. Swap gems to line up rubies, sapphires, emeralds and more; matches of four fire a line blast, fives forge a rainbow bomb, and chain cascades multiply everything.

🎯 How to Play

Tap a gem, then tap an adjacent gem to swap them (or swipe). Only swaps that make a match of 3 or more count — anything else swaps back free. Clear gems to score; the purple bar tracks your target. Run out of moves below the target and the quest ends. Use HINT when you're stuck — it costs 30 points.

⭐ Features

  • Endless level ladder with rising targets and move pressure
  • Line blasts, rainbow bombs and cascade multipliers
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
