About Rummy

The world's most-played card game, one-on-one against the computer. Draw from the stock or grab the discard, build sets and runs, and dump your deadwood. When your unmatched cards total 10 or less you can knock by discarding — lowest deadwood wins the difference. Meld everything and it's Gin, with a fat bonus.

🎯 How to Play

Each turn: click the stock (or the discard pile) to draw, then click one of your cards to discard it. Your melded cards glow green automatically — the DEADWOOD counter shows what you're still holding. Discard with deadwood of 10 or less to knock and end the round; if the CPU's deadwood is lower, it wins instead (and an undercut earns +10). First to 100 points takes the match.
