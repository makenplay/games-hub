About Sniper 3D Missions

Six contract missions on a busy city street. Each briefing describes exactly one person — the colour of their hat, their shirt, what they're carrying. Everyone else is an innocent civilian, and some are dressed almost the same. Find the right figure through your telescopic scope, steady the crosshair, and take the one shot you get.

🎯 How to Play

Move the mouse (or drag on touch) to sweep the scope across the street; the scope magnifies the crowd so you can read the details — hats, shirts, bags, umbrellas. Click to fire. Hit the described target to complete the mission and bank your time bonus; hit anyone else, or run out of time, and the mission fails. Later missions pack the street with near-identical decoys, so read the briefing twice and shoot once.

⭐ Features

  • A real magnifying scope over a living, walking crowd
  • Score = accuracy plus time bonus, best score saved
