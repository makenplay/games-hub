About Moto Stunts

A physics stunt-bike trial over three hand-built tracks of ramps, gaps and seesaw climbs. The throttle is easy; the art is in the air — lean to control your rotation, stick the landing, and flip for bonus points.

🎯 How to Play

Hold GAS (button, W, or ↑) to accelerate and brake/reverse with S. While airborne, lean back and forth with A/D or the LEAN buttons to rotate the bike: complete a full backflip or frontflip and land on both wheels for +150 points. Crash and you restart the level — your time and flips carry the score.

⭐ Features

  • Real two-wheel physics with airborne rotation control
  • Three tracks with ramps, gaps and a finish flag each
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
