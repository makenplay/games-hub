About Stickman Rope Swing

One button, pure momentum. Your stickman hero stands on a rooftop with a grappling rope and a long way to fall. Hold to fire the rope at the nearest anchor overhead, swing out over the streets, and let go at the perfect moment to soar to the next roof — all the way to the green finish tower.

🎯 How to Play

Press and hold anywhere on the canvas (or hold SPACE) and the rope hooks the best anchor in range; the swing builds speed as you drop through the bottom of the arc. Release while swinging upward-forward to launch. Clip a ⭐ star mid-flight for bonus points. Miss the landing and you splash down for a fall — the hero climbs back to the last roof and you try again. Three stages of widening gaps and trickier anchor lines; your best star count for each stage is saved.

⭐ Features

  • True pendulum physics — timing the release is everything
  • Three handcrafted rooftop stages with star pickups
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
