About Teen Patti

South Asia's beloved three-card game, played here with play-money chips against two computer rivals — Raja and Rani. Everyone antes up, gets three cards, and bets over three rounds. Read your hand, time your raises, and take the pot at the showdown. No real money, ever — just bragging rights.

🎯 How to Play

Press DEAL: everyone antes 10 chips and receives 3 cards (you can see yours, with its hand name). Each round you may CALL to match the current bet, RAISE to double it, or FOLD to drop out. The CPUs judge their own hands and bet, raise or fold in reply. After three rounds the remaining players show their cards — best hand takes the whole pot. If your chips hit zero, DEAL refills you with 500 on the house.
