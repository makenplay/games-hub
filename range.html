About FPS Shooting Range

Sixty seconds on a professional three-distance range. Targets flip up near, mid and far — the far ones are small, quick and worth a fortune — and in the second half a moving target starts crossing the back track. Pure aim, pure reflexes.

🎯 How to Play

Click or tap a target the instant it flips up; it drops again in about a second, faster as the round goes on. Near targets score 25, mid 50, far 100 and the moving target 150. Build a streak: five hits in a row opens a 5-second ×2 bonus window where everything scores double.
