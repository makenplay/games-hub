About Penalty Shootout

Football's cruellest drama, five kicks at a time. Place your penalties past a keeper who studies your habits, then pull on the gloves yourself and guess where the CPU will put theirs. Level after five? Sudden death.

🎯 How to Play

When shooting, click anywhere in the goal to aim — shots near the corners beat the keeper far more often but can fly wide or hit the post. When keeping, click the zone you want to dive to as the CPU runs up. Most goals after 5 rounds wins the trophy; sudden death decides a tie.

⭐ Features

  • Risk-and-reward aiming — corners score, corners miss
  • A keeper AI that reads your favourite corner, and a CPU shooter you must read in turn
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
