About Mini Golf

Six hand-built holes of pure putting craft. Bank shots off the walls, feather the ball through sand, and stay out of the water — every hole has a par, and the round is won stroke by stroke against it.

🎯 How to Play

Press and drag back from the ball to set direction and power (watch the guide line), then release to putt. The ball must be moving slowly to drop into the cup — a rocket will lip out. Sand drains speed, water adds a penalty stroke and returns the ball to the last safe spot.

⭐ Features

  • Six distinct holes with walls, sand traps and water hazards
  • Full par scoring across the round, with a saved best total
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
