About Archery

The ancient sport of kings, distilled into ten arrows. Stand on the range, draw your bow against a shifting wind and put every shaft as close to the gold as you can — then do it again when the target itself starts to move.

🎯 How to Play

Press and drag back from the bow to set your angle and power (the dotted line shows the arc), then release to shoot. The wind reading at the top pushes the arrow left or right in flight, so aim off to compensate. You have 10 arrows per round; the rings score 10, 8, 6, 4 and 2 from the centre out.

  • Real projectile physics with gravity and a changing wind
  • A moving-target second half that separates the true marksmen
