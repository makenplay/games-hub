About Tic Tac Toe

The quick-fire classic of X and O. Three in a row wins — sounds simple, but the unbeatable hard mode will test every trick you know.

🎯 How to Play

Tap a square to place your mark. Play against the computer on Easy, Medium or Unbeatable, or take turns with a friend on the same screen.

⭐ Features

  • Three AI levels — including a truly unbeatable one
  • Scoreboard for you, the computer and draws
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
