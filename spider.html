About Spider Solitaire

The deep-thinking cousin of Klondike, in its friendly 1-suit mode: two decks of spades across ten columns. Stack cards in descending order — any unbroken descending sequence travels together — and whenever a full run from King down to Ace forms, it lifts off the table. Clear all eight runs to win.

🎯 How to Play

Click a face-up card to select it and its whole descending tail, then click the column to move it onto (the target must be exactly one rank higher, or an empty column). Expose face-down cards to flip them. When you're stuck, DEAL ROW deals one fresh card onto every column — but only if no column is empty. Empty columns are gold: park a King there and build a full run on it.
