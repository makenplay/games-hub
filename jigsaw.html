About Jigsaw Puzzle

A classic jigsaw with a Maken Play twist: the picture is painted fresh by the game itself — a sunny lakeside scene with a cottage, mountains and trees. Pieces wait in the tray below the board; a faint ghost of the finished picture guides you, and any piece dropped on its own square locks into place with a green frame.

🎯 How to Play

Tap a piece in the tray to select it, then tap a square on the board to drop it there. Wrong square? Tap the piece again to pick it back up, or tap the tray to send it home. Stuck on a piece? HINT flashes the square it belongs to, PEEK shows you the whole picture while you hold it, and NUMBERS labels every piece with its square number. Pick 3×3 for a warm-up, 4×4 for the classic game, or 5×5 for a real challenge — your best time for each size is saved.

⭐ Features

  • Three grid sizes — 9, 16 or 25 pieces
  • Ghost guide, hold-to-peek, hint flashes and optional piece numbers
