About Platform Runner

The timeless run-and-jump adventure, rebuilt for the browser: three worlds of brick platforms, patrolling baddies, coin blocks and bottomless pits, ending at the flagpole. Stomp from above, never barge in from the side.

🎯 How to Play

Run with A/D or the arrow buttons and jump with SPACE/W/↑ (hold for higher). Land on a baddie's head to squash it for 100 points; side contact costs a heart. Bump ? blocks and bricks from below for coins. Reach the flag to clear the world — three worlds, three hearts.

⭐ Features

  • Three hand-built worlds with pipes, stairs and gap jumps
  • Stomp mechanics, coin blocks and a flagpole finish
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
