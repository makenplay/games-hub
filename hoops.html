About Basketball Hoops

Sixty seconds, one ball, one hoop — and a shooting spot that keeps changing. Find your arc from everywhere on the court, bank it off the glass when you must, and when the streak catches fire every basket counts double.

🎯 How to Play

Press and drag back from the ball to bend the dotted trajectory, then release to shoot. A clean swish scores 3, a basket off the rim or backboard scores 2. Three makes in a row puts you ON FIRE for double points — and in the second half the whole hoop starts sliding up and down.

⭐ Features

  • Real projectile physics with rim and backboard bounces
  • Random shooting spots and a moving rim in the second half
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
