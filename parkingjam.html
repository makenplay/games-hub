About Parking Jam Puzzle

The rush-hour traffic puzzle everyone loves. Your red car is boxed in on a packed 6×6 lot by cars, vans and trucks that can only slide forwards and backwards along their own length. Shuffle the whole jam around, one vehicle at a time, until the red car has a clear run at the green exit.

🎯 How to Play

Tap any vehicle to select it, then use the arrow buttons (or your keyboard arrows) to slide it one space at a time — horizontal vehicles move left/right, vertical ones up/down, and nothing can jump over another vehicle. The PAR score shows the fewest slides a perfect escape takes; every lot here is solver-verified, so an escape always exists. Beat all three lots and chase a perfect-par run.
