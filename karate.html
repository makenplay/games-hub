About Karate Fighting

A dojo duel in the classic side-view style. Sensei Ryu is patient, precise and punishes button-mashing — measure your distance, strike with purpose, and take two rounds to earn the black belt.

🎯 How to Play

Move with A/D or the arrow buttons, punch with J (fast, short), kick with K (long reach, heavy, slower), and block with L to reduce damage to a chip. Each round lasts 45 seconds or ends in a knockout; win two rounds to take the match.

⭐ Features

  • Real spacing combat — range, recovery times and blocking all matter
  • A CPU sensei who advances, retreats, blocks and strikes in patterns
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
