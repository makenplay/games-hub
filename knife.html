About Knife Hit

The precision thrower. A log spins — sometimes steady, sometimes surging, sometimes reversing — and you must bury a set number of knives in it without ever striking a blade already stuck there. Every fifth stage the log becomes a boss: a cheese wheel, a donut, a planet.

🎯 How to Play

Tap, click or press SPACE to throw the knife upward into the log. Watch the rotation pattern and pick your moment: a new knife needs clear wood around the impact point. Stick all the stage's knives to smash the log and advance. Apples on the rim are worth +25 when sliced.

⭐ Features

  • Endless stages with variable spin patterns and boss targets
  • Apple bonuses and a saved best-stage record
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
