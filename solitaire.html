About Solitaire

The classic Klondike patience game. Seven tableau columns, four foundations, one stock. Build the tableaus down in alternating red and black, free the buried cards, and stack every suit from Ace to King to win. This version uses friendly draw-1 rules and unlimited stock passes.

🎯 How to Play

Click the stock (top left) to flip a card to the waste. Click any face-up card to select it — a whole descending stack moves together — then click the column or foundation it should land on. Only a King can fill an empty column. Double-click a card to send it home automatically, or press AUTO-HOME to sweep every safe card to the foundations.
