About Dino Runner

The famous endless runner. Sprint across the desert as the T-Rex, leap over cacti, duck under pterodactyls and see how far you can get as the ground speeds up.

🎯 How to Play

Press SPACE or tap to jump, and the DOWN arrow to duck or fast-fall. Time double-jumps over wide cactus clusters and stay low when birds fly at head height.

  • Day-desert theme with smooth speed ramping
  • Jump, duck and fast-fall — the full runner moveset
