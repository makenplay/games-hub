About Helix Jump

The spinning-tower dropper. A ball bounces on a helix of ring platforms wrapped around a pole; you rotate the whole tower to line up the gaps and let it fall — level after level — while the red danger segments wait to end your run in one bounce.

🎯 How to Play

Drag sideways, use A/D or the spin buttons to rotate the tower. The ball bounces where it lands: dark segments bounce you safely, gaps let you fall through (+1 depth each), and red segments are instant death. Clear all platforms to finish the level and descend into a harder one.

⭐ Features

  • Endless levels with growing red-segment coverage
  • Combo falls build a fire drop that smashes one platform
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
