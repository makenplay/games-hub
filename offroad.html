About Offroad Jeep Driving

A single expedition trail with real offroad problems: mud pits that swallow your momentum, a river crossing that drags you sideways-slow, and a rock crawl where full throttle just breaks things. Four checkpoints, one finish line, one jeep that remembers every hard landing.

🎯 How to Play

Drive with GAS (D / →) and BRAKE (A / ←), tilting with the same pedals while airborne. Enter mud and water with speed — crawling in is fine, stopping in them is not. Land softly: drops and roof hits add damage, and 100% damage ends the run. Checkpoints repair 30% and bank your progress.

⭐ Features

  • Surface physics — mud, water and rock each drive differently
  • Damage model with checkpoint repairs across a 380 m trail
  • Free to play on mobile and PC — no download, no sign-up
